StepAway 隱私權政策

開發者：CouplePuzzle　｜　生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日

StepAway（以下稱「本遊戲」）是一款鼓勵多走路、少用手機的旅行收集遊戲。本政策說明本遊戲會使用哪些資料、用途為何，以及你可以如何控制。本遊戲不需要註冊帳號，也不會販售任何資料。

1. 只在你的手機上處理的資料

以下資料只在你的手機上計算與保存，不會上傳到我們的伺服器（我們沒有自己的伺服器）：

  • 步數：透過手機的計步感測器（「體能活動」權限）取得，用來換算遊戲里程。
  • 螢幕開關時間：若你開啟「使用情況存取權」，本遊戲只讀取螢幕開啟與關閉的時間點，用來換算體驗券；不會讀取你使用了哪些 App 或其內容。
  • 位置：只有在你點選「使用目前位置」時才會讀取一次，用來判斷離你最近的出發城市。遊戲只保存選定的城市，不保存座標。
  • 遊戲進度與設定：例如里程、收藏、語言與音效設定，保存在手機上。若你開啟 Android 的自動備份，這些資料可能依 Google 的備份機制備份到你的 Google 帳號。
  • 提醒通知：由手機在本機排程與產生，不經過任何伺服器。

2. 由第三方服務處理的資料

服務用途可能收集的資料
Google AdMob播放你主動選擇觀看的獎勵廣告廣告 ID、IP 位址、裝置與 App 資訊、廣告互動紀錄
Google User Messaging Platform在法規要求的地區（例如歐洲經濟區、英國）詢問你對廣告個人化的同意同意選擇

Google 時間伺服器取得網路時間，防止透過修改手機時間重置每日次數一般網路連線資訊（例如 IP 位址），不含個人資料

這些服務由 Google 提供並依其政策處理資料：Google 隱私權政策、Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料、Firebase 隱私權與安全性。

3. 你的選擇與控制

  • 步數、使用情況存取權、位置與通知權限都可以隨時在手機的「設定」中關閉；關閉後相關功能將停止運作，其餘遊戲功能不受影響。
  • 在遊戲的「旅人設定」中可以關閉「傳送錯誤報告」與「每日提醒通知」。
  • 你可以在手機設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID，或選擇停用個人化廣告。
  • 解除安裝本遊戲會刪除手機上的所有遊戲資料。

4. 資料保存

手機上的資料會保存到你刪除遊戲或清除資料為止。Crashlytics 的當機紀錄依 Firebase 的保存期限保存（目前為 90 天）。廣告相關資料的保存依 Google 的政策辦理。

5. 兒童

本遊戲並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，我們也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。如果你認為有兒童提供了個人資料，請與我們聯絡。

6. 政策變更

本政策若有更新，會在此頁面公布並更新生效日期。

StepAway Privacy Policy

Developer: CouplePuzzle　|　Effective date: October 11, 2026

StepAway (“the game”) is a travel-collection game that encourages walking more and using your phone less. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can control it. The game does not require an account and does not sell any data.

1. Data processed only on your device

The following data is calculated and stored only on your phone and is not uploaded to our servers (we do not operate any servers):

  • Step count: read from the phone’s step-counter sensor (Physical activity permission) to convert into in-game mileage.
  • Screen on/off times: if you grant Usage Access, the game reads only when the screen turned on and off, to convert screen-off time into experience tickets. It does not read which apps you use or their content.
  • Location: read once, only when you tap “Use current location”, to find the departure city nearest to you. Only the chosen city is saved, not your coordinates.
  • Game progress and settings (mileage, collections, language, sound) are stored on your phone. If Android Auto Backup is on, they may be backed up to your Google account under Google’s backup service.
  • Reminder notifications are scheduled and created on your phone, without any server.

2. Data processed by third-party services

ServicePurposeData that may be collected
Google AdMobShowing rewarded ads that you choose to watchAdvertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions
Google User Messaging PlatformAsking for consent to ad personalization where required by law (e.g. EEA, UK)Your consent choices

These services are provided by Google and process data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Firebase privacy and security.

3. Your choices

  • You can turn off the physical activity, usage access, location and notification permissions at any time in your phone’s settings; the related features stop working and the rest of the game is unaffected.
  • In the game’s Traveler Settings you can turn off “Send crash reports” and “Daily reminders”.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone’s settings.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all game data on your phone.

4. Retention

Data on your phone is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Crashlytics crash reports are kept according to Firebase’s retention period (currently 90 days). Advertising data is retained under Google’s policies.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page and revise the effective date.
